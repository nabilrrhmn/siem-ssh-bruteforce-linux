## Log Analysis Lessons for Blue Team Operations

### Key Takeaways
- Logs tell the attacker story even when attacks fail
- Failed events are often more important than successful ones
- Correlation across time and source is critical

### Common Indicators
- Repeated failures
- Short time intervals
- Unusual usernames or access patterns

### SOC Analyst Perspective
Effective detection depends on understanding patterns, not single events.

### Defensive Value
Consistent log analysis enables early detection and proactive defense.
